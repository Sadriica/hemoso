# Blink rate

## Why it matters

Blink rate is how many times you blink in one minute. A blink is a fast
closing and reopening of the eye. It lasts about 100 to 400 ms. HeMoSo counts
blinks during the 60-second eye check and compares them with your own earlier
checks. No source here says whether more or fewer blinks is worse, so a change
in either direction is only a note for you.
https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/113
http://vision.fe.uni-lj.si/cvww2016/proceedings/papers/05.pdf

## How spaceflight affects it

Sleep loss and a body clock out of step with the schedule add up in isolation
and confinement. How this changes your blinking is not settled. In the federal
study, blink monitors were weaker and less steady than eyelid closure at
following late answers on a reaction test.
https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-isolation-and-confinement/
https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/113

## Measurement steps

1. Start the eye check in the report.
2. Look at the camera in good light for 60 seconds.
3. Keep your face in view and your head steady. Glasses may change the result.
4. The camera image stays on the device. Only two numbers are saved.
5. Do the check at the same hour as yesterday.
6. Save the result.

What it cannot tell: dim light, glasses, glare and the camera angle can change
the count with no change in how you are. A closure longer than a blink is not
counted as a blink. HeMoSo has not validated this check on this device. A
longer sample predicts better than a short one, so one check is not a signal.
It reads your eyelids only. It says nothing about mood, stress or emotion.
https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/113
http://vision.fe.uni-lj.si/cvww2016/proceedings/papers/05.pdf

## Status and next steps

### no_data

HeMoSo needs 3 days of checks before it can compare you with yourself.

Do the check again tomorrow at the same hour. No one is notified for now.

### in_range

Your blink rate was in your usual range.

No action today. Do the check again at the same hour tomorrow.

No one is notified for now.

### outside_range

Your blink rate was different from your usual range.

Check the light and the camera angle, then do the check again after a short
rest. Check how you slept last night. Assumption.

Your CMO or flight surgeon confirms.

### watch

You blinked fewer than 6 or more than 30 times a minute. These two lines are
set for the demo, not taken from a source. Assumption.

Check the light and the camera angle, then do the check again tomorrow.

Your CMO or flight surgeon confirms.

## How to prevent problems

Keep the same sleep and wake times. Do the check at the same hour, in good
light, with the camera at eye level. Assumption.
