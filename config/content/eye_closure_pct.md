# Eye closure

## Why it matters

Eye closure is the share of the check in which your eyes are closed. Slow
eyelid closures, the "droops", follow how alert you are. In a federal study of
people kept awake for 42 hours, this measure followed their late answers on a
reaction test. HeMoSo uses a 60-second camera version, so it is a rough
estimate and not a medical test.
https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/113

## How spaceflight affects it

Sleep loss and a body clock out of step with the schedule add up in isolation
and confinement. When alertness drops, the eyes may stay closed longer.
https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-isolation-and-confinement/
https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/113

## Measurement steps

1. Start the eye check in the report.
2. Look at the camera in good light for 60 seconds.
3. Keep your face in view and your head steady. Glasses may change the result.
4. The camera image stays on the device. Only two numbers are saved.
5. Do the check at the same hour as yesterday.
6. Save the result.

What it cannot tell: dim light, glasses, glare and the camera angle can change
the number with no change in how alert you are. A narrowed eye, for example
when you smile, can also change it. HeMoSo has not validated this check on this
device. A longer sample predicts better than a short one, so one check is not a
signal. It reads your eyelids only. It says nothing about mood, stress or
emotion.
https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/113
http://vision.fe.uni-lj.si/cvww2016/proceedings/papers/05.pdf

## Status and next steps

### no_data

HeMoSo needs 3 days of checks before it can compare you with yourself.

Do the check again tomorrow at the same hour. No one is notified for now.

### in_range

Your eyes were closed as much as in your usual range.

No action today. Do the check again at the same hour tomorrow.

No one is notified for now.

### outside_range

Your eyes were closed more than in your usual range.

Check how you slept last night. Check the light and the camera angle, then do
the check again after a short rest. Assumption.

Your CMO or flight surgeon confirms.

### watch

Your eyes were closed more than 15 % of the check, a line set for the demo, or
more than your usual range.

Protect your sleep tonight and do the check again tomorrow. Assumption.

Your CMO or flight surgeon confirms.

## How to prevent problems

Keep the same sleep and wake times. Do the check at the same hour, in good
light, with the camera at eye level. Assumption.
