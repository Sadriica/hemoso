## How to get there

1. Profile icon, top right, then **My Team**.
2. On the team page, the **Project** tab.
3. Blue **Edit** button.
4. Turn the **Lock** to ON while editing, so nobody else edits at the same time.
5. **Save Progress** often. Nothing is public until you press Submit for Judging.

Images are allowed (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB). Videos and documents are not:
they go as links.
