# Pitch and submission

Bio 1 owns the script, slides and video; Bio 2 owns the submission page and the
AI-use disclosure. Both rehearse the story.

## Script (2 minutes, `submission/script.md`)
Problem (with a NASA fact) → who uses the solution and when → one concrete
story through the demo → what it does not do (suggests conditions as probabilities, never a certainty) → evidence and its
limits (only numbers the team can rerun) → maturity stated honestly → next step.

## Slides (7 at most, `submission/slides.md`)
1. Title: project, team name, members, challenge.
2. The problem, with a NASA source.
3. How it works.
4. The demo story (screenshots).
5. Who sees what.
6. Evidence and what it does not show.
7. Next steps and NASA data used.

## Video (30 s at most, English subtitles, `submission/video.md`)
Second-by-second script and the screenshot list for Eng 2 (`docs/DEPLOY.md`).

## Submission page (`submission/submission.md`)
Name, summary, demo link, project link, description, NASA data (from Phys),
other data and tools with licenses, and the AI-use disclosure written from every
`team/*/LOG.md`, one clear sentence per use. AI-generated images carry a visible
watermark; AI-generated content does not use or modify NASA branding.

Use the `human-writing` skill throughout. No figure without a source or a test behind it.
