# Human writing

Everything in the repository is in English and must read as written by a
person on the team, not by an assistant.

## Do
- Short, direct sentences. Main point first.
- Concrete words: numbers with units, names, mission days.
- For the astronaut: second person ("your pulse", "rest more").
- One steady register: a serious university team.

## Do not
- Filler: "Certainly!", "Of course", "In summary", "In conclusion",
  "It's worth noting", "It is important to highlight", "In today's world",
  "Let's dive in".
- Empty words: "robust", "innovative", "seamless", "cutting-edge",
  "game-changer", "leverage", "delve", "holistic", "synergy", "revolutionary".
- "Moreover" and "Furthermore" opening paragraph after paragraph.
- Emojis, stacked exclamation marks, bold in every sentence.
- Lists of three adjectives out of habit. Em dashes in every paragraph.

## Never
- Mention AI, models or assistants in the product, the repository or the
  commits. The only exception is the mandatory AI-use disclosure field on the
  submission page, which Bio 2 writes.
- Signatures, "generated by", co-authorship lines.

Before delivering any text, read it again and cut every sentence that says
nothing concrete.
